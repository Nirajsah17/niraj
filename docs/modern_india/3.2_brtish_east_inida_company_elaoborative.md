# British East India Company (EIC) – Story Format

---

## 🏁 Beginning: A Trading Dream (1600)

In **1600**, a group of English merchants got permission from **Queen Elizabeth I** to trade in the East.  
This company was called the **East India Company (EIC)**.

👉 Think of them as *businessmen, not rulers* — at least in the beginning.

---

## 🚢 First Step in India: Surat (1608)

The British first landed at **Surat** in **1608**.

They wanted Mughal permission, so they sent **William Hawkins** to Emperor **Jahangir’s court**.

❌ But problem:
- Portuguese were already powerful
- Hawkins failed to get permission

---

## ⚔️ Turning Point: Battle of Swally (1612)

The British didn’t give up.

In **1612**, they defeated the Portuguese in the **Battle of Swally (near Surat)**.

👉 This impressed Jahangir

✅ Result:
- In **1613**, British got permission to open a factory at **Surat**

---

## 🤝 Smart Diplomacy: Thomas Roe (1615)

Now British used *brain instead of war*

- **Sir Thomas Roe** came as ambassador
- He impressed Jahangir

🎁 Got:
- **Royal Farman (trade rights)**

👉 Now British trade became stable

---

## 🏗️ Expansion Phase

### 📍 South India (Madras)

- In **1639**, **Francis Day** got land
- Built **Fort St. George (Madras)**

👉 First strong base of British in India

---

### 📍 West India (Bombay)

- **1661** → Got Bombay from Portugal (dowry)
- **1668** → Given to EIC for just **£10/year**

👉 Cheap deal, big advantage 😄

---

### 📍 East India (Bengal – The Gold Mine)

- **1651** → Factory at **Hugli**
- **1690** → **Job Charnock** built **Calcutta**
- **1696** → Fort William

👉 Bengal = richest region → became main focus

---

## 📜 Game Changer: Farman of Farrukhsiyar (1717)

This was a **turning point** 🔥

Mughal Emperor **Farrukhsiyar** gave:

### Benefits:
- Duty-free trade in Bengal  
- Use of **Dastaks (free passes)**  
- Trade allowed in Gujarat & Hyderabad  

👉 British became **too powerful economically**

⚠️ Problem:
- They misused Dastaks
- Indian traders suffered

---

## 🧵 Trade Story: What Did British Buy?

### 1. Cotton Textiles (MOST IMPORTANT)

Types:
- Calico  
- Dimity  
- Muslin (very fine, from Dhaka)

👉 India = world leader in textiles

---

### 2. Silk (STK Trick)

Main centers:
- Kasimbazar  
- Murshidabad  
- Banaras  
- Ahmedabad  
- Lahore  
- Multan  

---

### 3. Indigo (Blue Gold 💙)

- Used for dye in Europe
- Farmers forced to grow it

❌ Problems:
- Low payment  
- Soil damage  

🔥 Result:
- **Indigo Revolt (1859–60)**

---

### 4. Saltpetre

- From **Bihar**
- Used in **gunpowder**
- High demand in Europe

---

### 5. Opium (Afeem)

- Produced in **Bihar**
- Exported to **China**

👉 Became a major profit source

---

## 😔 Dark Side: Exploitation

### Dadni System

- British gave advance money to weavers
- Forced them to sell at low prices

👉 Result:
- Artisans became poor  
- Local industries weakened  

---

## 🧠 Easy Timeline Trick

1600 → Company formed  
1608 → Surat entry  
1612 → Swally battle  
1613 → Surat factory  
1615 → Thomas Roe  
1639 → Madras  
1668 → Bombay  
1690 → Calcutta  
1717 → Farrukhsiyar Farman  

---

## 🎯 Final Understanding (1 Line)

👉 British came as **traders**, became **powerful traders**, and slowly turned into **rulers**.
